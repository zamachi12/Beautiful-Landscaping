About

One designer, start to finish. No hand-offs, no guessing who to call.

Beautiful Landscapes is a woman-owned landscaping and irrigation company founded in Round Rock in 1996. Owner Esther Ortiz is a TCEQ licensed irrigator, and the same designer who walks your property is the one who manages the crew and answers your calls until the job is done. We work on homes, apartment communities and commercial properties across the Austin metro, with free consultations, written estimates and a warranty on our work.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Beautiful Landscapes is fully insured, and our irrigation work is done under Texas irrigator license LI# 0016669, issued by the TCEQ, which regulates irrigation in Texas.

Which areas do you serve?

Round Rock, Austin, Cedar Park, Leander, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Hutto and the surrounding Austin metro.

Do you offer emergency sprinkler repair?

Yes. Call (512) 743-5850 any time, day or night. Emergency projects can also be scheduled on Sundays for an additional fee.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. Consultations and written estimates are free, and we offer transparent pricing with no surprises.

What deposit do you require?

Projects typically start with a 50% deposit, with the balance due on completion. Financing is available through LightStream.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. We offer tailored maintenance plans with visits scheduled on the same day and time each week, so your landscape looks its best year-round.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship and provide a warranty so you have peace of mind long after the project is finished.
